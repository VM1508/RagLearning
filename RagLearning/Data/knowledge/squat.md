# Back Squat

## Technique
Brace your core before you unrack the bar. Keep the bar over mid-foot for the whole rep. Push your knees out in line with your toes and descend until the hip crease is below the top of the knee.

## Knee pain when squatting
Knee pain during squats is often caused by the knees caving inward or by a sudden jump in training volume. Reduce the load, slow the tempo to three seconds on the way down, and strengthen the hips with glute bridges. Box squats to a comfortable depth let you keep training while the knee settles. See a physiotherapist if pain is sharp or lasts more than two weeks.

## Programming
Beginners can squat three times per week using 3 sets of 5 reps and add 2.5 kg per session while form stays clean.
