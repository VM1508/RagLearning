# Deadlift

## Setup
Stand with the bar over mid-foot. Grip just outside the legs, pull the slack out of the bar and keep the spine neutral before the bar leaves the floor.

## Lower back safety
Rounding the lower back under heavy load increases injury risk. Keep the bar close to the shins and stop the set when you can no longer hold a neutral back. The Romanian deadlift (RDL) builds hamstrings with less lower back fatigue.

## Programming
Most lifters progress well pulling heavy once per week, for example 1 to 3 working sets of 3 to 5 reps, because the deadlift is very fatiguing.
