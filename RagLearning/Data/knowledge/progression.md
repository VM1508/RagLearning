# Progressive Overload

## Principle
Strength improves when training stress increases gradually: more weight, more reps, or more sets over time.

## Estimated one rep max
The Epley formula estimates a one rep max (1RM) as weight multiplied by one plus reps divided by thirty. Tracking estimated 1RM week to week shows whether you are getting stronger without testing a true max.

## Plateaus
When progress stalls, first check sleep and nutrition, then change rep ranges or add a deload before adding more volume.
