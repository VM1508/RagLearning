# Recovery

## Sleep
Aim for 7 to 9 hours of sleep. Poor sleep reduces strength, slows recovery and makes sessions feel harder.

## Deload
A deload week cuts volume by about 40 to 50 percent while keeping intensity moderate. Take one every 4 to 8 weeks or when performance drops for two sessions in a row.

## Soreness
Muscle soreness a day or two after training is normal. Light movement, walking and adequate protein help it resolve.
