# Bench Press

## Technique
Retract your shoulder blades, keep your feet planted, and lower the bar to the lower chest with elbows at roughly 45 degrees.

## Shoulder pain
Shoulder pain on bench is usually from flared elbows or a too-wide grip. Narrow the grip slightly, tuck the elbows, and add face pulls and rotator cuff work.

## Stalled bench press
If the bench press has stalled for three weeks, add a second weekly bench day, use paused reps, and strengthen triceps with close-grip bench.
